# Changelog

## 0.3.1

- Hand and arrow now use the same continuous full-word sweep as the dot.
- Cursor tips and the dot's visible edge sit one pixel below the text range at every size.
- Compact 280px controls with shorter labels and no slogans; recovery guidance and the tutorial remain available.

## 0.3.0

- Continuous leading-edge dot sweep with a short fading trail and clean line returns.
- Highlight groups of 1–4 words with per-word timing and inclusive passage boundaries.
- Six standard cursor/outline colors, saved alongside existing appearance preferences.
- Interactive practice walkthrough: select a word, change speed, and try play/pause without altering the page.

## 0.2.0

- Smooth browser-managed motion for cursor, highlight, and outline modes, using the RSVP Reader follow-along timing.
- Same-line fragments from emphasis/links are merged so formatted words do not interrupt the animation.
- Clean line returns; pause freezes in-flight motion and resume continues without rewinding.
- Pointing-hand, dot, and arrow cursor options, with size and stroke controls.
- First-use walkthrough, a persistent help button, and backward-compatible preference migration.

## 0.1.0

- Initial Chrome/Brave extension with article detection, start/end word selection, WPM controls, three guide modes, and automatic scrolling.
